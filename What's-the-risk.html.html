What’s the Risk?

Drug and alcohol addiction, also known as substance use disorder, can happen to anyone. While it’s hard to predict who will become addicted and who will not, there are a variety of factors that impact our behaviors.

Studies have found that the more risk factors a teen has, the greater the likelihood they will engage in risky behaviors. Teens with more protective factors and a resilient personality are better able to cope with risk factors and life challenges and are less likely to be involved in risk-related behaviors.
