Serving size math

Everything on a label is for one serving. Eat two servings and you double every number, including the %DV. This is the most common label mistake.

The 5/20 rule

You don't need to memorize Daily Values. Just look at the %DV:

5% or lessLow
6–19%Moderate
20% or moreHigh

Then ask: is this a nutrient I want more of (fiber, vitamin D, calcium, iron, potassium) or less of (saturated fat, sodium, added sugars)? For each line below, choose both.

Ingredient detective

Ingredients are listed in descending order by weight: the heaviest ingredient comes first, the lightest comes last. Ingredients in parentheses are what a sub-ingredient is made of.
