Family Roles in Codependency

When one person struggles, the whole family adapts.

Codependency is a pattern where your sense of safety or worth depends on managing someone else’s behavior. In families affected by addiction, people often fall into predictable roles to survive the stress. Learn the roles here, then you’ll find them in a real person’s story.
