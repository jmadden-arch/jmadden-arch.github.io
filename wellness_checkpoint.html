Habits of Mind

Habits of Mind are the ways you think and act when something is challenging. Your examples can come from this class, another subject, or your wellness in general (sleep, stress, friendships, nutrition). Choose 3 habits. For each one, rate yourself and answer four short prompts.

FitnessGram and wellness summary

Write at least 6 sentences that respond to all four prompts.

  • All four tests: reflect on your performance across every fitness and wellness test.
  • Class connections: connect your data to the wellness concepts discussed in class.
  • Motivation and potential: were you fully motivated? Did you perform to your potential? What could boost your drive next time?
  • Future goals: how will these results shape your upcoming physical health and fitness targets?

SMART goal summary

Review your goal, analyze your results, and plan your next steps. Write at least 6 sentences.

  • Progress and connections: reflect on your progress, connecting it to class content and your future goals.
  • Analyze results: did you accomplish your goal? Why do you think you got these results?
  • Adjustments: if you didn’t meet your goal, what will you change? If you did, what is your new goal?
